# Page 1: Monthly Bookkeeping

**URL:** /services/monthly-bookkeeping
**Breadcrumb:** Services / Monthly Bookkeeping

## Hero

**Headline:** Clean books, every month, without thinking about it.

**Subhead:** For solopreneurs who want their books handled on a steady monthly cadence — categorized, reconciled, reported, and ready whenever you need them.

**CTA:** Book a free consultation →

## Who monthly bookkeeping is for

You're running a solo business and you need your books maintained consistently — not scrambled together once a year. Maybe you've tried doing it yourself and it fell off. Maybe your business is growing and the admin burden is getting real. Either way, you want a system that runs quietly in the background so you can focus on actual work.

## What's included every month

- **Transaction categorization** — Every business transaction reviewed and assigned to the right account, every month.
- **Bank & credit card reconciliation** — Accounts reconciled so your books match your statements, no mystery variances.
- **Monthly financial reports** — Profit & loss and balance sheet delivered in plain English — no accounting degree needed.
- **QuickBooks Online setup & maintenance** — Your chart of accounts kept clean, rules maintained, and software optimized as you grow.
- **Unlimited email support** — Ask questions throughout the month and get same-day answers — no per-minute billing.
- **Year-end tax-ready package** — Financials organized and ready for your CPA every January, no catch-up scramble.

## How we'll work together

**1. Onboarding (week 1)** — We connect your QuickBooks, review your chart of accounts, and make sure everything is set up cleanly before any monthly work begins.

**2. Monthly close (by the 10th)** — By the 10th of each month, last month's books are closed, reconciled, and reports delivered to your inbox.

**3. Ongoing support** — Email me anytime with questions. Quick quarterly check-ins to review trends and flag anything worth your attention.

## Common questions

**How many transactions does monthly service cover?**
Transaction volume is one of the factors in your custom quote. Most solopreneurs fall in the range that's standard — we'll confirm on our call.

**Do I need QuickBooks Online?**
QBO is what I recommend and am certified in, but if you're on Xero, Wave, or something else, let's talk — I can usually make it work.

**What if my books are currently behind?**
Very common. We'll start with catch-up bookkeeping to get current, then transition you to the monthly plan. Both get quoted together.

**Is there a long-term contract?**
Month-to-month. If the fit isn't right, you can stop any time — though most clients stay long-term.

# Page 2: Catch-Up Bookkeeping

**URL:** /services/catch-up-bookkeeping
**Breadcrumb:** Services / Catch-Up Bookkeeping

## Hero

**Headline:** However far behind you are, we can fix it.

**Subhead:** Three months behind. Twelve months behind. Never started. The books have gotten away from you — and you need them cleaned up without judgment, without a lecture, and without dragging it out.

**CTA:** Book a free consultation →

## Who catch-up bookkeeping is for

You're behind. Maybe tax season is coming and you're panicking. Maybe a lender asked for financials and you don't have them. Maybe you've been meaning to deal with it for months and it's finally time. Whatever got you here, you need someone to take the pile of receipts, bank statements, and half-started QuickBooks files and turn them into clean, usable books — fast.

## What catch-up bookkeeping includes

- **Full historical review** — Every transaction for the catch-up period reviewed, categorized, and recorded properly.
- **Account reconciliation** — Bank and credit card statements reconciled month by month until your books match reality.
- **Chart of accounts cleanup** — QuickBooks categories reorganized so everything makes sense going forward.
- **Historical financial reports** — P&L and balance sheet for the catch-up period — useful for taxes, loans, or your own clarity.
- **Transition plan** — Optional handoff to monthly service so you don't fall behind again.
- **Zero judgment** — No lectures, no "you should have done this sooner." Just the work, done properly.

## How catch-up works

**1. Scope the mess** — On our consultation, we'll look at what period needs catching up and the state of your current records. I'll send a flat-fee quote based on scope.

**2. Gather records** — You share bank statements, credit card statements, and any receipts or records through secure document upload. I'll tell you exactly what I need — no overwhelming checklists.

**3. The work gets done** — Most catch-up projects complete in 2-4 weeks. You get status updates, not silence. At the end, you have clean books and organized financials ready to use.

## Common questions

**I'm multiple years behind. Can you still help?**
Yes. Multi-year catch-up is common, especially around tax filings. We'll scope it year by year and quote accordingly.

**What does catch-up cost?**
Catch-up is priced as a flat-fee project based on the period, transaction volume, and state of existing records. We'll scope and quote during the free consultation.

**What records do I need to have?**
At minimum, bank and credit card statements for the catch-up period. If you have QuickBooks data, even half-finished, that helps. Don't worry if it's messy — that's normal.

**How fast can you turn it around?**
Most projects close in 2-4 weeks once records are in hand. Larger multi-year projects take longer but can be prioritized if there's a tax deadline.

# Page 3: Tax-Ready Financials

**URL:** /services/tax-ready-financials
**Breadcrumb:** Services / Tax-Ready Financials

## Hero

**Headline:** Your CPA's new favorite client.

**Subhead:** Year-end financials prepared exactly the way tax preparers want them — so your filing is fast, your CPA isn't chasing you for documents, and January doesn't eat your whole month.

**CTA:** Book a free consultation →

## Who tax-ready financials are for

You already have a CPA or tax preparer — or you're about to hire one — and you want year-end financials handed over in a format they can actually use. No mystery entries, no "what is this transaction?" emails, no scramble for missing receipts. Just a complete, organized package that makes your tax filing a non-event.

## What's included in your year-end package

- **Full-year P&L and balance sheet** — Clean, reconciled financials covering the full tax year, ready for your CPA to work from.
- **Categorized expense summary** — Expenses organized by Schedule C or business return categories so nothing falls through.
- **Mileage & home office documentation** — Supporting documentation organized for common solopreneur deductions.
- **1099 prep support** — Contractor payment reports pulled and ready for 1099 filing by the deadline.
- **CPA handoff** — Secure transfer of everything your preparer needs — and answers to their follow-up questions.
- **CPA referrals if needed** — Don't have a tax preparer? I can connect you with CPAs I trust who work well with solopreneurs.

## How the year-end package works

**1. Review your year** — We assess the state of your books. If you've been a monthly client, we're nearly done already. If not, we scope what catch-up is needed first.

**2. Prepare the package** — I reconcile, close out the year, pull supporting documentation, and compile everything into a format your CPA will thank you for.

**3. Hand off and follow through** — Package goes to you and (if you want) directly to your CPA. I stay available to answer their questions so you don't have to play middleman.

## Common questions

**Do you file taxes?**
No — I'm a certified bookkeeper, not a CPA. I prepare tax-ready financials that your CPA or tax preparer uses to file. If you don't have one, I'm happy to refer you.

**When should I start this process?**
Ideally in January or early February. The earlier we start, the less pressure during filing season. Late filers (closer to April or on extension) are also welcome.

**What if my books are a mess?**
We'll start with catch-up bookkeeping to clean up the year, then prepare the tax-ready package on top of that. Both get scoped and quoted together.

**Can you work directly with my CPA?**
Yes. With your permission, I handle all communication with your CPA during tax prep — you stay out of the email chain unless decisions need your input.
